# Story Arc (four acts) — all beats beyond Ch.2 are `proposed`

## Act I — The Discovery (Earth) · Ch.1–5
- **Ch.1 The Discovery** *(locked, written)* — Groen announces; Emma is credited; Rich congratulates.
- **Ch.2 Celebrating the Success** *(locked, written)* — Home. The "big decision" deferred a night.
- **Ch.3 The Decision** — Emma and Rich talk. Rich lets her choose. Sam volunteers (seed of the triangle).
- **Ch.4 Training** — Team forms; Kaplan, Sam, Emma. Rich's Capcom certification. Jameson sets comms rules.
- **Ch.5 Goodbyes / Launch** — Emma's seed vial, Rich's garden promise, "Come back to me" / "I will."
  **Branch point B1** (fan): what does Emma bring from home? (vial / grandmother's letter / Rich's recording)

## Act II — Terra (Landfall) · Ch.6–12
- Landing, first footprints, wonder. Video calls with delay. The microbial life confirmed (script). Public joy.
- The **root-sync pulse**: plants are timing themselves to *Earth's* day. Sam isolates the isotope signature.
- Kaplan & Jameson decide to withhold the anomaly. Emma writes Rich a code-in-plain-sight message (botany terms).
- **Midpoint — The Orchard:** a grid of identical trees, planted. Terra's life was *seeded*, long ago.
  **Branch point B2** (fan): who should Emma tell first — Rich / Sam / Kaplan?

## Act III — Signal (Earth ⇄ Terra) · Ch.13–19
- Rich decodes Emma's message; fights the program from inside. Fans ("Terra Watch" citizen scientists) crack the
  pattern in public data — in-universe, fans' findings reach Rich.
- Sam holds back a critical result (temptation). Emma's trust fractures; Rich's trust holds.
- Jameson moves to recall or lock down. The team splits: stay and study vs return and warn.
  **Branch point B3** (fan): Does the truth go public? (full release / controlled / withheld)

## Act IV — Return or Roots · Ch.20–end
- Terra's "seeders" didn't plant us a gift — they planted a **mirror**: Terra was prepared as a home *for the
  species it expected to arrive*. We were guided here. The real question: invited, or harvested?
- Emma's choice: stay, return, or send the seed home. Rich's choice: accept the risk and go to her.
  **Branch point B4** (fan): the ending's shape (reunion on Terra / reunion on Earth / separation with a legacy).
