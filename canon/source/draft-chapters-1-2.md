Chapter 1: The Discovery


February 10th, 2043. 8:47am. NASA Headquarters, Washington DC. 

The room is filled with a palpable excitement as scientists and NASA officials eagerly await the start of a presentation. 

The room is buzzing with anticipation, as this is a momentous occasion - a new planet had been discovered 6 years ago, but this presentation will be about the latest discoveries from the latest exploration mission. 

The group begins taking their seats around the conference table where there is a holographic display of the planet Terra. 

Terra was discovered to be only 7% larger than Earth, filled with liquid water, and a thick atmosphere composed of almost identical gases as Earth. The planet has a slightly warmer average temperature with three times the amount of coastlines. Terra is being explored as an opportunity to become a new home for humanity.

A slender man sits at the head of the table. He has a smile on his face that could not be removed even if he tried. The man is NASA Planetary Science Director Dr. Timothy Groen and he is about to share information that will change the course of humanity. 

“Ladies and gentlemen”, Dr. Groen said with an excited quiver. “The data from our latest transmission on planet Terra is conclusive.” “We have found it, the most Earth-like planet! All of you already know how similar the planet is, but the latest data has confirmed the presence of several types of vegetation with extremely similar chemical properties as those on Earth.

The room erupts in excitement and chatter. 

Dr. Emma Baptiste, a young and talented botanist, sat at the table, her eyes shining with excitement as she eagerly took notes as Dr. Groen talked about the various samples brought back from the new world. She was the lead scientist on the vegetation sample project. Emma’s heart swelled with pride as she listed, her mind racing with the possibilities of this new world and the impact it could have on the future of humanity. 
Dr. Groen said, “I can’t end this presentation without recognizing Dr. Emma Baptiste. Her dedication to this project has brought about one of the largest discoveries ever made. Dr. Baptiste, your hard work has come to the best final conclusion. Congratulations!”
The room once again erupted with excitement and applause.
Emma gave a shy smile, looking down and to the side. She wasn’t big on attention.
Emma was filled with a mix of pride and excitement. She had always dreamed of discovering something significant in the field of botany and now her hard work was being recognized. She couldn’t help but to take a quick look at a colleague across the table and flashed a big clinched teeth smile to show her extreme excitement.
As the conference ended and everyone started to clear out of the room, Emma sat for a moment to soak everything in. Once she decided to get up and walk out of the room, she heard someone calling her name. 
She turned to see a man jogging towards her with a broad smile on his face. He was a tall guy with an athletic build, broad shoulders, and the skin color of a mocha latte. 
"Congratulations, Emma! You did an amazing job with the vegetation sample project," Richard said, his tone filled with excitement. “It’s a big discovery, and you should be proud.” Richard then leaned in to kiss her on the check.
Emma felt a warm flush rise to her cheeks as she looked into his eyes. She was always happy to receive praise from her colleagues, but coming from Richard, it meant even more. They had worked together for several years and she admired his intelligence and passion for their work.
"Thanks, Rich. It was a team effort," Emma replied with a smile.
"Still, you deserve all the credit. Let's celebrate tonight," Rich suggested with a twinkle in his eye.
"I'd like that," Emma replied

They walked down the hallway.
, their minds already starting to race with plans for their celebration.

Chapter 2: Celebrating the Success

Emma and Rich lived in a small town just outside of Houston, down a quiet subdivision with big yards. Their house was a cozy one-story brick cottage, surrounded by a lush garden filled with native Texas wildflowers and all sorts of tropical plants.
As Rich pulled into the driveway, he couldn't help but smile at the sight of his wife's handiwork. Emma's passion for botany was evident in every inch of their yard, and the house was no exception.
He walked up the front steps and opened the door, stepping into a living room filled with lush green plants of all shapes and sizes. Emma had turned her love of botany into a veritable art form, decorating their home with micro biomes, hanging baskets, and terrariums of all shapes and sizes.
"Hey, you're home," Emma said, looking up from her workbench as Rich entered. "How was your day?"
"It was good," Rich replied, crossing the room to give her a kiss on the cheek. "But it's always better now that I'm home with you."
Emma smiled, her eyes softening as she looked into his. "Same here," she said. "How was the conference?"
"It was great," Rich said. "But I have to admit, I was a little distracted the whole time, thinking about you and the news about the new planet."
Emma nodded, her expression turning serious. "I know," she said. "It's a big decision. But I think we need to talk about it. We need to decide what's best for us, both professionally and personally."
Rich nodded, taking her hand in his. "You're right," he said. "But before we do that, let's just enjoy the evening. We can talk about it tomorrow."
Emma smiled, feeling a sense of comfort in his touch. "Sounds good," she said, snuggling into his embrace.
They stood there for a moment, taking in the peace and quiet of their home. Despite the uncertainty of the future, they were grateful for each other, and for the love they shared.
