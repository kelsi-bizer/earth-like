Sci-Fi

The book is called Earth-Like

“Earth Like” about humanity finding an earth like planet. There is a young couple in love and the woman is a scientist who will be on the first human team going to the new planet. The couple struggles with their soon to be long distance relationship.

Dr. Jessica is a brilliant young scientist in her late 20s. She has always been fascinated by space and the possibility of finding other habitable planets. She has dedicated her life to studying the universe and has worked her way up the ranks in her field. Despite her love of science, she also values her personal relationships and has a close bond with her boyfriend, Adam. She is excited about the opportunity to be a part of the first human team to explore a new planet, but is also nervous about being away from Adam for a long period of time.

Dr. Jessica has a husband, Adam. 

Adam is a kind and supportive young man in his late 20s. He is a skilled engineer and works alongside Jessica in the laboratory. Despite his own career aspirations, he is deeply in love with Jessica and wants nothing more than to support her dreams. He is proud of her accomplishments and understands the importance of her work, but the prospect of a long-distance relationship is difficult for him. He tries to stay positive and stays in touch with Jessica through video chats while she is away on the new planet.


Dr. Sam is a colleague of Jessica and Adam at the laboratory. He is a brilliant scientist in his early 30s with a quiet, serious demeanor. Despite his professional demeanor, he has a secret crush on Jessica and admires her intelligence and dedication to her work. He tries to hide his feelings and remain professional, but often finds himself wondering what it would be like to be in a relationship with her. He volunteers to join the team on the new planet in hopes of getting to know Jessica better, but struggles with the fact that she is in a committed relationship with Adam.
