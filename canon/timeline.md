# Master Timeline

Dates from the draft are `locked`; everything else is `proposed`.

| Date | Event | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| 2037 | Terra (exoplanet) discovered. | locked ("6 years ago") |
| 2037–2042 | Remote observation; robotic sample-return missions bring back vegetation samples. | open |
| 2043-02-10 | **Discovery presentation**, NASA HQ, Washington DC. Dr. Groen announces vegetation with near-Earth-identical chemistry; credits Dr. Emma Baptiste. | locked |
| 2043-02-10 (eve) | Emma & Rich celebrate at home near Houston; the "big decision" (Emma going) is deferred a night. | locked |
| 2043 (spring) | Crew selection; Emma named to the first landing team. Sam volunteers. | proposed |
| 2043–2044 | Training; Rich cleared as Mission Control Capcom (Earth side). | proposed |
| 2044 | Launch. | proposed |
| 2044–2046 | Transit (travel mechanism TBD — see `canon/open-questions.md`). | open |
| 2046 | Landing on Terra; first footprints. | proposed |
| 2046–2047 | Surface operations; first microbial/ecological anomalies. | proposed |

**Release clock:** the real-world release calendar maps story-time to wall-clock time in
`storyboard/tracker.yaml` (`release_date` per beat). Characters may only know what has been released
(see `docs/architecture.md` → *Knowledge gate*).
