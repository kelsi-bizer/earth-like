# Rich — Backstory

**Locked:** Emma's long-time colleague and husband; kisses her on the cheek, celebrates her wins, comes home to a house
full of her plants, and says he was distracted all day "thinking about you and the news about the new planet."

**Proposed:**
- Navy-trained pilot who moved to ground systems after a training accident ended his flight-status for long-duration
  missions — he *could* have gone to Terra and didn't (a quiet wound; why he never asks Emma to stay).
- Grew up in Houston's Third Ward; father a shrimp-boat mechanic, mother a schoolteacher.
- Learns to keep Emma's plants alive; the garden becomes his way of talking to her across the gap (fan-facing motif:
  "Rich's Garden Log").
