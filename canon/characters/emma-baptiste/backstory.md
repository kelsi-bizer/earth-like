# Emma Baptiste — Backstory

**Locked:** Young, talented botanist. Lead scientist on the vegetation sample project. Doesn't love attention — gives a
shy smile and looks down when praised. Turned her home into a living lab: micro-biomes, terrariums, hanging baskets.
Married to Rich.

**Proposed:**
- Raised by a grandmother who ran a nursery on the Louisiana coast (hence the surname; Creole family). Hurricanes
  taught her that *plants are what come back*. She became a botanist because "roots remember."
- First in her family to earn a doctorate. Met Rich at a mission-prep fellowship — "worked together for several years."
- Her great fear isn't space; it's being *wrong in public*. Terra's too-perfect chemistry is the first thing she
  has ever suspected is wrong in a way that matters.
- Keeps a seed from her grandmother's garden in a locket-size vial. It travels to Terra (a plot object).
