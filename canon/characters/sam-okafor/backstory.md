# Sam Okafor — Backstory
**From outline:** Brilliant scientist, early 30s, quiet and serious. Secret crush on Emma, hides it, stays professional.
Volunteers for Terra "to get to know her better" but struggles with her being committed to Rich.

**Proposed:** Born in Lagos, raised in Houston; chemistry prodigy; first to detect *the isotope signature* that makes
Terra's chemistry look "engineered". Unsent drafts of letters to Emma are a plot device (fans can unlock them).
His arc is the moral test: he finds the truth first and is tempted to hold it back to keep the team (and Emma) on Terra.
