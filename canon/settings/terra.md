# Terra

**Locked (Ch.1):** 7% larger than Earth · liquid water · thick, near-identical atmosphere · slightly warmer average
temperature · **three times Earth's coastline** · vegetation with extremely similar chemistry to Earth's.

## Proposed
- **Look & feel:** Teal-green canopy, silver-grey "salt-moss" plains, long tidal flats. Two small moons, one close and fast.
- **Why three times the coastline:** fractal archipelagos; the coast is where the first anomalies appear.
- **Flora anomaly:** plants show *synchronized* behavior (root-signal pulses) that follows Earth's day-length, not Terra's.
- **Sound:** wind in canopy sounds like a distant crowd.
- **Base:** *Landfall Camp* on a river delta. Habitat modules named after Earth cities.
