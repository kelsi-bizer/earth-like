Answer these and I'll lock them into canon. Defaults are what the repo currently assumes.

   enough that signals lag **hours-to-days** and transit takes ~2 years (generation-ship-free, cryo-light).
2. **Name reconciliation.** Chapters use *Emma Baptiste / Rich / Dr. Groen*; outline uses *Jessica / Adam / Sam*;
   script uses *Emma / Jack / Kaplan / Jameson*. Default: Emma, Rich, Sam, Groen, Kaplan, Jameson all exist; Jessica & Adam are retired.
3. **Rich's job.** Chapter 1 has him as Emma's long-time NASA colleague; script has him as a pilot. Default: Mission
   systems engineer & certified pilot → Earth-side Capcom (the voice that talks to Emma).
4. **Is the relationship a marriage?** Chapter 2 says "wife"; outline says "boyfriend". Default: married.
5. **The twist.** Default `proposed` answer in `canon/story-arc.md` (Terra was *seeded*). Keep, change, or hide from AI agents?
6. **Real NASA?** Default: in-universe agency is a **fictional** analogue for anything that appears on real
   websites/social media (see `docs/safety-and-disclosure.md`).
7. **Rating / tone limits** for AI agents (violence, romance, grief content).
8. **Platforms** you want first (X, Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Bluesky, a website?).
