# EARTH-LIKE — Premise & Series Bible

**Genre:** Sci-fi drama / slow-burn mystery. **Format:** Novel + serialized transmedia ("living story").
**Core relationship:** Emma (on Terra) and Rich (on Earth) — love across 4+ light-years of delay and silence.

## Logline
In 2043, humanity confirms Terra — a planet 7% larger than Earth, with liquid water and almost identical
biochemistry. Botanist Dr. Emma Baptiste, whose work proved the plants are chemically *too* familiar, joins the
first human team to land there. Her husband Rich stays behind as the voice in her ear — until the signals from
Terra stop matching what Emma says she's seeing.

## The big question
Terra is not just Earth-like. **Why is it so Earth-like?** Coincidence, convergence, or intent — and who
planted the idea (and the seeds) first?

## Pillars
1. **Love under distance.** The emotional spine. Every sci-fi reveal must cost the couple something.
2. **Familiar made strange.** Terra's plants, weather and coastlines should feel like home, then slightly wrong.
3. **The audience is in the story.** Fans are in-universe participants (see `docs/fan-canon-rules.md`).
4. **Honest fiction.** The world leaks into real channels, but never deceives people about what it is
   (see `docs/safety-and-disclosure.md`).

## Tone
Warm, grounded, wondering — *Contact* / *Arrival* / *The Martian* warmth, with a growing *Annihilation* unease.
Hard-ish sci-fi: plausible science, human stakes. No villains-for-villainy's-sake; institutions are the pressure.

## Canon tiers (how we label truth)
| Tier | Meaning | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| `locked` | Published or author-decided. Never contradicted. | Author only |
| `open` | Decided in the bible but unreleased; may change before release. | Author |
| `fan-influenced` | Outcome set by a fan vote/action; recorded in `memory/canon-ledger.jsonl`. | Fans, within author-defined branch points |
| `proposed` | Suggested by the development team/AI; needs author approval. | Author approves |

> **Everything marked `proposed` in this repo is a suggestion drafted from your outline — not decided canon.**
